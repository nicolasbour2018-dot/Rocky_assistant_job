Job title: DATA ANALYST

Location: Paris, France

Job reference #: 33732

Contract type: Permanent

Language requirements: Fluent in French and English


At Eni, we are looking for a Data Analyst to join Eni Plenitude France. In this role, you will work closely with cross-functional teams, including Marketing, Finance, Sales, Operations and Customer Service, to transform data into actionable insights that drive business performance. You will play a key role in analysing, interpreting and enhancing data to support commercial growth, optimise the customer experience, address business needs and enable strategic decision-making, ultimately contributing to the company’s operational efficiency and long-term success.


About Plenitude


Plenitude is an energy company that integrates the retail business and renewable generation with self-consumption and electric mobility solutions, providing energy and gas to more than 10 million homes and businesses across Europe, which offers a range of great professional opportunities for the whole team. This partnership represents a major European project integrating the renewable energy business with the retail business, which will enable us to progressively increase our offering of services, infrastructure, and green energy for our customers, providing our team with greater visibility and professional growth.


Main Responsibilities


  • Data Collection and Processing: Extract, transform and structure data from a variety of internal and external sources.
  • Data Analysis: Conduct quantitative and qualitative analyses to identify trends, customer behaviours and performance drivers.
  • Data Visualisation and Reporting: Develop and maintain interactive dashboards (Power BI) and establish and monitor business KPIs.
  • Advanced Analytics: Contribute to the development of models, forecasts and predictive analyses.
  • Optimisation and Recommendations: Provide actionable insights to improve processes, marketing campaigns and the customer experience.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with technical teams, including IT, developers and data scientists, while partnering with business departments to understand analytical needs and business objectives.
  • Data Culture: Promote data-driven decision-making and support internal teams in the effective use of data and KPI management.


Skills And Experience Required


  • Bachelor's or Master's degree (BSc/MSc) in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, or a related field.
  • Proficiency in data visualisation tools, particularly Power BI.
  • Good knowledge of data analysis languages and tools, including SQL, Python and R.
  • Experience with Big Data and/or Cloud environments (Azure, Google Cloud Platform or AWS) would be an advantage.
  • Significant experience in data analysis.
  • Strong analytical skills, attention to detail and intellectual curiosity.
  • Ability to translate complex analyses into clear, meaningful and impactful insights for different audiences.
  • Collaborative mindset and ability to work effectively across multiple functions.
  • Well-organised, self-motivated and capable of managing priorities independently.
  • Results-oriented, with strong influencing and communication skills.
  • Fluent in English.


How To Apply


  • Applications are only accepted through our online application system.
  • Please upload your CV in English.


About Eni


Eni is a global energy company operating in 61 Countries, with over 30,000 employees. Originally an oil & gas company, it has evolved into an integrated energy company, playing a key role in ensuring energy security and leading the energy transition. Eni's goal is to achieve carbon neutrality by 2050 through the decarbonization of its processes and of the products it sells to its customers. In line with this goal, Eni invests in the research and development of technologies that can accelerate the transition to increasingly sustainable energy. Renewable energy sources, bio-refining, carbon capture and storage are only some examples of Eni's areas of activity and research. In addition, the company is exploring game-changing technologies such as fusion energy – a technology based on the physical processes that power stars and that could generate safe, virtually limitless energy with zero emissions.


Working at Eni


At Eni we believe in enterprising people, capable of making a difference and making their contribution with passion and innovation, to respond to the global challenges of the energy transition. For us, the skills and attitudes of each individual, continuous training, and diversity and inclusion are fundamental. We promote flexible ways of working with particular attention to well-being, welfare and work life-balance. Eni will evaluate applications considering plurality and diversity as sources of enrichment. If your application is assessed to be among those most in line with the required profile, you will be contacted to continue the selection process. Whatever your ambition, at Eni you can find the tools to make it happen.
